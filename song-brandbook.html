01 · Manifesto

Why we exist

"We are not in the coffee business.
We are in the business of pause."

松 means release. To let go of tension. To exhale fully. SŌNG was built around that single idea — that in a city that never stops, the most radical thing we can offer is stillness.

We serve specialty coffee with quiet confidence. Our spaces are designed to slow the eye. Our language is warm but never performative. Every surface, every cup, every moment of interaction carries the same intention: nothing is rushed. Nothing is loud. Nothing is unnecessary.

We are not a trend. We are a practice.

04 · Audience

Who we serve

Who

25–40. Works in design, tech, or creative fields. Reads in cafés. Owns fewer, better things. Travels for food experiences.
What they seek
Quality without the lecture
They know good coffee. They don't need it explained. They want to feel understood, not educated. They share when the aesthetic earns it.
What we offer
Permission to stay
No background music above 60db. No loyalty-point pressure. No menu items named after trends. Just excellent coffee and space to breathe.
05 · Brand Voice

How we speak

01
Unhurried
We write the way we pour — slowly, without rushing to a point. Short sentences. Space after each thought. No exclamation marks. Ever.
02
Precise
We name the origin, the process, the altitude. Specificity is a form of respect — for the grower, the bean, and the person holding the cup.
03
Warm, not cute
Our warmth comes from confidence, not performance. We are friendly but never giggly. We never write "cozy vibes" or "coffee o'clock."
04
Bilingual at heart
Chinese and English live together naturally. A single character — 松 — carries more meaning than a paragraph. We use both languages as equals.

09 · Rules

Do & don't

Always
Use lowercase in casual copy — it reads quieter
Name origin country and region on every item
Leave generous white space in all layouts
Use Clay Ember as the single accent color only
Natural light photography only — slightly underexposed
Pair Chinese characters with English when decorative
Never
Use "artisan," "craft," or "curated" — meaningless now
Add exclamation marks or emoji to brand copy
Use more than two typefaces in one composition
Photograph cups from directly above
Name drinks after seasons, moods, or trends
Use pure black #000 or pure white #FFF anywhere

Good brands sell coffee.
Great brands create rituals.
